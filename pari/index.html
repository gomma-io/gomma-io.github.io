Pari Privacy Support

Share costs together. Stay even.

Pari is an iPhone app for two people who share a life and its costs. Siamo pari means “we’re even”.

Pay with your card and Pari asks one question: split it, your treat, theirs, or just yours. One calm line tells you both where you stand.

Payments you don’t share never leave your phone. No ads, no tracking.

Pari is in private testing on TestFlight.
