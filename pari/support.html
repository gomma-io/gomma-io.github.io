Support

Questions, bugs, or something that felt off? Email hello@gomma.io. A person reads every message.

Payments aren’t being picked up

Open Shortcuts, find the Pari automation, and check it is set to Run Immediately. For Apple Pay, the card also needs notifications switched on in Wallet (open the card, then ⋯, then Notifications).

A payment shows the wrong shop

In Pari, go to Settings, then Auto-import payments, then Recent captures, tap Copy all and send it to us. Remove any account balances first.

Deleting your account

In Pari, go to Settings, then Delete account. If your partner still uses Pari they keep the shared history, where you appear as “Former partner”. Otherwise the household and everything in it is deleted.
