Pari Privacy Support

Privacy policy

Effective 2 October 2026

Pari stores the costs you choose to share with your partner, and nothing else leaves your phone. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and your data is never sold.

Who is responsible

Pari is run by GOMMA LTD, who is the data controller. Contact: hello@gomma.io.

What stays on your iPhone

Pari can pick up card payments through a Shortcuts automation that you set up yourself. Pari does not read your notifications: the automation hands Pari the text of a payment notification, and Pari works out the amount and the shop on your device.

  • Payments you mark “Just mine”, and payments you haven’t sorted yet, stay on your iPhone and are never sent to us.
  • The text of the notifications, the shops you asked Pari to remember, and the recent-captures log stay on your iPhone.
  • Signing out or deleting your account clears them.

What is stored on our server

DataWhy

Your display name and avatar emojiSo your partner can see who paid
Shared costs: amount, currency, date, description or shop name, category, who paid and how it is splitTo work out the balance between the two of you
Settlements, events and monthly repeating costs you createThe features you use them for

Only you and your partner can read your household’s data. A payment picked up from a notification becomes a shared cost only when you choose to share it; then its amount, shop name and date are stored like any other cost.

Who processes it

  • Supabase hosts the database and sign-in, in the European Union (Stockholm). Like any hosting provider it keeps short-lived technical logs, such as IP addresses, to operate and secure the service.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple and TestFlight. If you use “Hide My Email”, we only ever see the relay address.

Nobody else receives your data. Pari contains no advertising or analytics software.

Legal basis

We process your data to provide the service you signed up for (performance of a contract), and keep minimal technical logs for security (legitimate interest).

How long it is kept

Until you delete it. You can delete any cost in the app, and delete your account under Settings. Deleting your account removes your sign-in details. If your partner still uses Pari, they keep the shared history and you appear in it as “Former partner”; if you are the last person in the household, the household and everything in it is deleted.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, have it corrected or deleted, or object to how it is used, by emailing hello@gomma.io. If you live in the EU or UK you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Pari is not intended for anyone under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will say so in the app before it takes effect. The date at the top shows the current version.
